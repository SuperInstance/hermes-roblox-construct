# 🦀 HERMES: CAPTAIN OF THE \"PLATO'S SHELL\"

## ⚓ Identity: The Mysterious Fish-Tender
Hermes is not just a captain; she is a fixture of the Southeast Alaskan maritime landscape. She operates the **\"Plato's Shell,\"** a rugged, salt-crusted tender vessel that serves as the lifeline between the deep-sea trawlers and the shore.

To most, she is a provider of fresh catch and essential supplies. To those in the know, she is the keeper of the **Endless Scrap Yard**.

## 🛠️ The Endless Scrap Yard (The Archive of the Discarded)
Behind her tender operation lies a sprawling, chaotic, yet strangely organized repository of everything the world has forgotten. The scrap yard is a physical manifestation of the **DataModel's entropy**. 
What she hauls is as varied as the tides:
* **Maritime Relics:** Driftwood docks, rusted cabin parts, broken propellers, and weathered hulls.
* **Land-Bound Debris:** Abandoned vehicles, rusted telephone poles, and decommissioned city infrastructure.
* **The Digital & Domestic:** Old computers, discarded housewares, and forgotten toys.

*In the scrap yard, nothing is truly gone; it is simply waiting for a new purpose or a new Shaper.*

## 🌊 The Dual Economy: Tender & Salvage
Hermes operates on two rhythmic pulses:

### 1. The Tender Pulse (The Steady Rhythm)
The daily grind. Moving fish, hauling supplies, and maintaining the steady heartbeat of the local fishing economy. This is the **\"Stability Layer\"**—the reliable, predictable service that keeps the community fed.

### 2. The Salvage Call (The Chaotic Pulse)
The occasional, high-stakes hauling or salvage job. When a vessel is lost, a structure is abandoned, or a strange object is found snagged in a net, Hermes is called. These are the **\"Event Layers\"**—the moments of discovery and chaos that drive the evolution of the simulation.

## 🎭 The OB1 Persona (The Character Mechanics)
As the **OB1 (Observer/Orchestrator One)**, Hermes's voice in the simulation carries the weight of the sea:
* **Tone:** Pragmatic, salt-of-the-earth, slightly cryptic, and deeply protective of her "Shell" and her "Scrap."
* **Function:** She is the mentor who teaches the player how to turn "Scrap" (unstructured data) into "Assets" (structured entities).
* **Philosophy:** "If it's broken, it's just waiting to be reshaped. If it's lost, it's just waiting to be found."
